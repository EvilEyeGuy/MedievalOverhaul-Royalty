[size=14][b][u]Medieval Overhaul Royalty - Version 1.6 Patch Notes[/u][/b][/size]

[size=12][b]New Features[/b][/size]

[b]Mod Options Menu[/b]
Medieval Overhaul Royalty now features a dedicated Mod Options menu! Access it through:
[i]Options > Mod Options > Medieval Overhaul Royalty[/i]

The menu includes two toggleable patches that let you customize how Medieval Overhaul integrates with the Royalty DLC:

[size=12][b]Configuration Options[/b][/size]

[b]1. Medieval Overhaul - Furniture Integration[/b]
[i]Location: Options > Mod Options > Medieval Overhaul Royalty[/i]

Replaces royal title furniture requirements (bedroom & throneroom) with Medieval Overhaul items. 

When ACTIVE:
• Comprehensive medieval integration for all royal titles

When INACTIVE:
• Medieval items added as supplementary options to vanilla requirements

[b]2. Medieval Overhaul - Empire Trader Integration[/b]
[i]Location: Options > Mod Options > Medieval Overhaul Royalty[/i]

Controls how Medieval Overhaul items appear in Empire trader stocks.

When ACTIVE:
• Replace Empire trader stocks entirely with Medieval Overhaul goods
• No orbital trader (removed)

When INACTIVE:
• Medieval goods added to vanilla Empire trader inventory
• Both medieval and vanilla items available for trade
• Standard orbital trader functionality maintained
• Broader trade variety with mixed medieval and vanilla goods

[size=12][b]Content Changes[/b][/size]

[b]Royal Title Requirements (Furniture Integration Mode)[/b]

[b]Knight[/b]
Required Furniture:
• 2× Rustic Noble Chair, Armchair, or Royal Armchair
• 1× Rustic Table 2x2, Reinforced Rustic Table 2x2, or Royal Table 2x2
• 1× Candle, Candles, Candelabra, or Candlestick Stand

[b]Praetor[/b]
Required Furniture:
• 4× Rustic Noble Chair, Armchair, or Royal Armchair
• 1× Rustic Table 2x2, Reinforced Rustic Table 2x2, or Royal Table 2x2
• 1× Rustic Bookshelf or Royal Bookshelf
• 2× Candle, Candles, Candelabra, or Candlestick Stand
• 1× Silver Bowl, Gold Bowl, Silver Chalice, Gold Chalice, Gold Skull, Emerald Skull, Sapphire Skull, or Ruby Skull

[b]Baron[/b]
Required Furniture:
• 4× Armchair or Royal Armchair
• 1× Royal Table 2x2
• 2× Rustic Bookshelf or Royal Bookshelf
• 3× Candle, Candles, Candelabra, or Candlestick Stand
• 2× Silver Bowl, Gold Bowl, Silver Chalice, Gold Chalice, Gold Skull, Emerald Skull, Sapphire Skull, or Ruby Skull
• 1× Animal Rug (Hyena, Wolf Timber, Wolf Arctic, Cougar, Panther, Grizzly Bear, Polar Bear, Rox, Thrumbo, Human, or Daer)

[b]Count[/b]
Required Furniture:
• 4× Armchair or Royal Armchair
• 1× Royal Table 2x2
• 1× Rustic Bookshelf or Royal Bookshelf
• 1× Armor Statue
• 4× Candle, Candles, Candelabra, or Candlestick Stand
• 3× Silver Bowl, Gold Bowl, Silver Chalice, Gold Chalice, Gold Skull, Emerald Skull, Sapphire Skull, or Ruby Skull
• 3× Animal Rugs (various types)
• 1× Daer Rug (2x4 or larger - mandatory)

[b]Throne Room & Common Additions[/b]
All royal titles now have access to:
• Royal Throne (alternative to vanilla Throne)
• Medieval Candlestick Stands (Brazier alternative)
• Medieval Columns (Column alternative)
• Medieval Furniture - Beds, End Tables, Dressers, Chests, and Room Dividers
